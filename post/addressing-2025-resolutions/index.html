Addressing my 2025 resolutions

Since we’ve gotten to the topic of offloading, here goes ;)

In 2025, I made some resolutions. Although more than 1.5 years have passed, I still want to address them.

Q1 Goals

  1. Lose Weight - going to a wedding

I think I was able to, but of course got it all back and more after the trip. I did get into running though, after running around the UP Academic Oval (maybe and idea for a future post - my 2025 running phase).

  1. Brush up on Chinese - going to HK
    • Not really required to get around, but one time I tried ordering bubble tea and couldn’t even say what I wanted

I was shy so I didn’t even get to use Cantonese 😭 except for this one time when I was asking for 馬拉糕. I did use some Mandarin on our China trip though, so maybe I just was more confident with Mandarin (or I didn’t have the choice to speak English)

  1. Try to eat out less
    • Lines up with Goal 1 and 2, since I’m going on a trip and need to save up

Utter failure

The rest of the year

  1. Record myself talking every day
    • I want to be able to tolerate my voice
    • Also to improve my speaking because I get lazy pronouncing stuff

I didn’t get to do it either 😭 I probably didn’t start at all. I hope to change that again this year though with Obsidian (another post idea!).

  1. Reduce (useless) screen time
    • Keep my phone on greyscale

YouTube is crazy addictive 😭 I have been watching slop all day. Luckily I was able to save myself the last couple hours of today.

I like the greyscale idea though. I did it for my phone again just now.

  1. Consume less, create more
    • This has been my goal for a while, but perfectionism always gets in the way
    • Try to write and publish every day (charles.thyck.top)
    • Time for music covers?

We already know I failed at that in 2025 😂 but I am trying now in mid-2026. The music covers idea is crazy though

  1. Keep track of my money
    • Before I just put whatever is left from credit card bills into my savings, but now I want to actually try to hit a savings percentage as a goal
    • 60% is probably attainable considering my living situation

I just kept doing what I was doing before 🙂 I now have less savings

Bonus

  1. Move out

It did NOT happen 😭 especially not now with my upcoming MSc

  1. Greet people on their birthdays
    • First step is finding out what their birthdays are
      • I only know mine because I have to use it to fill out forms (I still forget sometimes)
      • I only know one (1) of my sisters’ birthdays because it’s the same as mine
      • I only know Jherzey’s because she’ll get mad otherwise

I got through the first step (knowing their birthdays), but I’m shy 🥺
