Ergodox EZ

In 2022, I received an Ergodox EZ as a Christmas gift. It felt pretty nice at first, but I had the idea to also learn Dvorak with it, which made me abandon it for a few months.

From a draft in June 2023:

For the past few months since I’ve had this keyboard, I never really became productive in it. Pretty sad, to be honest.

I think my mistake was learning too much at once. Aside from having to learn the ortholinear key layout, I had to learn the Dvorak layout, how to navigate my computer using the keyboard, etc. Heck, I had to relearn how to use Vim!! It was crazy.

I tried giving up Dvorak once and switched to QWERTY, but the layout was already ingrained in me. I thought it was already impossible to use QWERTY on this keyboard. I switched back to Dvorak, and the cycle of struggle continued.

Now, for the past few days, I’ve been using the default QWERTY layout by Ergodox, modifying some of the thumb keys (and of course, my swapcaps). I haven’t been changing anything else other than the timing parameters and stuff. It still feels a bit too sluggish for me.

For the actual typing, it was painful to unlearn Dvorak, but I did get through it. After unlearning Dvorak, the next hurdle was making my brain realize that it’s the same layout. That was also tough. I had kind of picked up bad habits on the regular keyboard, especially for the B and Y keys. I crossed over a lot for those, and now it’s impossible to do that.

3 years later, I’m still doing great with it. Looking back, I feel sad that I didn’t continue learning Dvorak, but I didn’t really need a new layout if it meant losing Vim motions. Plus, it was actually making my right wrist hurt worse when I’m typing on a regular keyboard (rather than making both of my wrists hurt on QWERTY).

I’m probably going to need a quieter keyboard when I go work at an office, though. They’re brown switches, but I probably bottom out a lot, and I definitely annoy my coworkers with it when I’m typing during huddles.

*This is post #8 for my first 100 Days To Offload challenge.
